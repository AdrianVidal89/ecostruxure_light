{# ---------- Requirements & Use Cases ---------- #}
{# Contextual guideline / help (spec Fase 10b) #}

Need / Imposes (MVP)

A capability the POC must deliver to be viable — the minimum viable product. Non-negotiable.

Use Case

A concrete scenario an actor (e.g. Operator) wants to achieve. Use cases group the requirements that make them possible.

Requirement

A specific, verifiable condition the solution must meet. Requirements are attached to use cases and validated by tests.

  • · Imposes (MVP) — mandatory for the MVP.
  • · Optionally — valuable but not required.
  • · Preferred but not required — nice to have.

Support / Help

When in doubt about scope or classification, contact the POC lead or an admin. A requirement is “validated” once all its linked tests pass or are skipped; a use case is “approved” once all its requirements are validated.
